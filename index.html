The business question

Losing employees is expensive: recruiting, onboarding and lost knowledge all add up. The goal of this project was to answer three questions for an HR team: Where is attrition concentrated? Which factors go together with leaving? Where should HR act first?

Dashboard preview

Key findings

1Early tenure is the riskiest

Attrition is highest during the first 1–2 years of employment and falls as tenure grows.

2Retention improves with tenure

Employees with longer service leave far less often, so the first years are the critical window.

3Overtime stands out

30.53% of employees working overtime left, compared with 10.44% of those who don’t, about 2.9 times higher.

4Lower income, higher attrition

Attrition is highest in the lowest income group and decreases as income rises.

Recommendations

  • Review overtime workloads, especially in the job roles and departments with the highest attrition.
  • Strengthen onboarding and support in the first two years, for example mentoring and regular check-ins.
  • Review pay competitiveness for the lowest income group.

These patterns show association, not proven cause. They tell HR where to look first, and they should be tested with further analysis such as exit interviews or statistical testing.

How I built it

  • Data preparation: cleaned the raw HR dataset and created a cleaned table for reporting.
  • Calculated columns: age groups, tenure groups, salary groups and a risk level to make patterns easy to compare.
  • DAX measures: attrition rate, retention rate, turnover rate, headcount and early turnover.
  • Interactivity: department and job role slicers, plus drillthrough from the overview to a job role deep-dive page.
  • Design: a consistent colour palette, KPI cards on top and a short key findings panel for quick reading.

Limitations and next steps

  • Some groups are small (for example, Human Resources has only 63 employees), so their rates should be read with care.
  • The data is a snapshot, so it cannot show how attrition changes over time.
  • Next: repeat the analysis in SQL and test whether the overtime and income differences are statistically significant.

4Gəlir aşağıdırsa, attrition yüksəkdir

Attrition ən aşağı gəlir qrupunda ən yüksəkdir və gəlir artdıqca azalır.

Tövsiyələr

  • Overtime yükünü yenidən nəzərdən keçirmək, xüsusilə attrition ən yüksək olan vəzifə və departamentlərdə.
  • İlk iki ildə adaptasiya və dəstəyi gücləndirmək, məsələn mentorluq və müntəzəm görüşlər.
  • Ən aşağı gəlir qrupunda maaşın rəqabətə davamlılığını yoxlamaq.

Bu nəticələr əlaqəni göstərir, səbəbi isə sübut etmir. Onlar HR-a hara baxmalı olduğunu göstərir və exit interview və ya statistik test kimi əlavə analizlə yoxlanmalıdır.
